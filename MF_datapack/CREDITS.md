### Inspirational Artists and Works

This data-resource-pack was heavily inspired by Raspmary's wonderful modpack "Raspberry Flavoured"

The modpack is really very good! If you want a cleaner version of this datapack go check it out
https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/raspberry-flavoured

The only reason I left behind raspmary's modpack was because I wanted a forever world, and I 
wanted it to be forever-vanilla compatible.

The textures for this pack where heavily inspired by JermsyBoy. After he stopped updating his pack, I started
updating it for my own use. Eventually, I went in and re-did everything myself, but a lot of the textures I 
made where inspired directly from his work.
His original texture pack: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/texture-packs/a-little-taste-of-jerm
He also has a youtube channel, and has some stunning builds: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCICnSwa3x5sHTWmTP6YO30w
